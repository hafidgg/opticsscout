# SEO Content Roadmap — Proposals Pending Approval

**Status: none of this is written yet. Every item below is a proposal for the user
to approve individually before any content is drafted.** This document doesn't add
pages, guides, or products — it's the output of Phase 3 of the 2026-09-25
SEO audit: using well-known, generic keyword-pattern shapes for the outdoor optics
niche (not our own GSC data, which is still too thin to be useful) to find real
structural gaps in what we already have.

## Where the gap is

All 3 categories are currently symmetric: 2 products + 1 head-to-head comparison
each. Every existing page targets **bottom-of-funnel, model-specific intent**
("Vortex Diamondback HD 8x42 — Specs, Price, and Who It's For", "X vs Y"). Nothing
targets the **broader, higher-volume query shapes** that typically surround this
niche before someone has picked a specific model: "best binoculars for birding
under $X", "8x42 vs 10x42", "spotting scope vs binoculars", "budget rangefinder for
hunting". That's a real, structural gap across all three categories, not a content
count problem.

**Birding Optics is the thinnest relative to its own demand shape.** Binoculars are
the broadest, highest-volume category of the three (used for birding, hunting,
sports, travel — a wider audience than spotting scopes or rangefinders), yet our
two products are both the same magnification (8x42), and one of them (Nikon Monarch
M5) has no sourced price at all. A category with the widest real-world query variety
currently has the least price/spec diversity to support that variety. Spotting
Scopes and Rangefinders are comparatively better matched to their narrower,
more specialized demand curves.

## What this roadmap deliberately does NOT propose

- No "best binoculars under $X" page that would need products we don't have.
- No content requiring new merchant/affiliate relationships beyond what's already
  live (Amazon, MidwayUSA).
- No generic/thin programmatic pages — every idea above is a single, specific,
  editorially-real page, not a template applied at scale.
- No rewriting of the 9 existing pieces — this is additive-only, and nothing here
  should be read as a critique of what already exists.
